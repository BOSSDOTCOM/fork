# Training-data provenance and Fork's positioning

Survey of 2026-11-10. Every engine's data and nets are **OFF-LIMITS** as
Fork training data, whatever their licence (KB rule 7, DESIGN.md
training-data decision 1). This file classifies them so the owner can judge
which results are evidence for Fork's own-data route.

## The rule applied

Training-data decision 1, as amended by the owner on 2026-11-10
(phase-4 95f49c7): **games against other engines are not Fork-pure data.**
In such a game half the moves and the result come from the opponent. Such
positions and results never enter Fork's training data; at most they form a
separate, labelled lineage, and only by an owner decision.

So an engine is counted as **own self-play** here only if its rated
version's data comes from its own self-play games, labelled by its own
search or nets. An engine that keeps only its own side's moves from games
against other engines (Dragon, Ethereal) still counts as mixed.

## Classes

| Class | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **own** | own self-play, labelled by the engine itself (a caveat is noted where the lineage or a date is uncertain) |
| **Lc0** | Lc0 data, or data relabelled by an Lc0 net, possibly mixed with other sets |
| **mixed** | own data mixed with another engine's data, with games against other engines, or a lineage seeded by an Lc0-trained net |
| **unknown** | not stated in anything the survey opened |

## Engines whose results count as evidence for Fork's route

Fork's route is own self-play only, chained to its own bootstrap eval, no
Lc0 data, and no games against other engines.

**Clean evidence** (own self-play in the rated version, no stated caveat):
- in the top 20: PlentyChess (#5, 3773), Viridithas (#16), Stormphrax (#17)
  and Hobbes (#18);
- then Astra (#21-22, from random weights), Clover (#26), Halogen (#29),
  PZChessBot (#32), JustBot (#35), Minke (#36-38) and Velvet (#36-38);
- Ginkgo (#39), Tcheran (#40-41), RubiChess (#23-25), Igel (#31), Seer
  (#43-44) and Renegade (#45-48);
- and Lc0 (a different paradigm, MCTS on a GPU).

**Evidence with a caveat:**
- Integral 8: the Vine data's date is an inference.
- Caissa: its earliest lineage touched a Stockfish net.
- Raphael: its first bootstrap positions came from CCRL games, since rotated
  out.
- Tarnished: its teacher's origin is unstated.
- Devre 7: older Leela data may remain.

**What transfers.**
- Search, time-management and testing results transfer whatever the data;
  the evaluation's error profile can still shift margins.
- Network-architecture and training-recipe results from Lc0-trained engines
  are weaker evidence for Fork. Their labels are deep and from a different
  engine, and their volumes are in the hundreds of billions (ZigQueen 131 B,
  Triumviratus about 270 B usable).
- Of the recipe findings in comparison.md, the own-data engines support:
  - the result-weighted final stage (Hobbes, Raphael, Halogen, Minke,
    Tcheran, Tarnished, Seer);
  - stepwise label budgets (Hobbes, Raphael, Tcheran, Minke);
  - data purges (Minke, Raphael);
  - king buckets grown with data (Raphael, Tarnished, Hobbes, Tcheran);
  - threat inputs (Hobbes, PZChessBot, Halogen, Devre).

  Ethereal's +42.7 is mixed-data evidence: its stage-2 data includes
  Stockfish-to-move positions.

## Positioning

- **Own data reaches the top 5.** PlentyChess is #5 at 3773, 30 Elo behind
  #1 Stockfish (8-CPU entry), on its own self-play. Of the top 10, five use
  Lc0 data. The Lc0 project calls its data "the best source of training
  data for NNUE engines" (questionnaire 2026-11-06).
- **The switching cost is real but recoverable.**
  - Lizard's first self-play net lost 33 at STC and 36 at LTC against its
    Lc0 net.
  - PlentyChess's first own net cost about 15 (S2).
  - Astra needed 21 generations from random weights to beat its Lc0 net,
    then gained +99 at release.
  - Quanticade's self-play net beat its last Lc0 net by +14.7 LTC.
  - Fork never had an Lc0 net, so it pays no switching cost. Its gap is in
    data volume and label depth.
- **Data volume at the own-data peers:**
  - Halogen 14.8 B through v16 (24.2 B now);
  - Raphael about 30 B;
  - Hobbes up to 40 B in one 3.0 net;
  - Astra 12 B, Minke 23 B, Tarnished 22 B, PlentyChess 15 B+;
  - Renegade 8.4 B, Ginkgo 6.7 B.

  Fork trains on about 1.5 B saved positions per retrain. Peers grew king
  buckets and wider layers only as their data grew (comparison.md H3),
  which matches Fork's bake-off finding that richer nets lost at Fork's
  volume.
- **TCEC's NNUE Guideline.** Ginkgo states compliance and rofChade
  (≥ 3.105) declares non-compliance because of Lc0-derived data, in their
  TCEC questionnaires. The Guideline's text was not opened in this survey
  (UNVERIFIED). Fork's own-data route keeps it on the compliant side
  whatever the text says on Lc0 data.
- **A label for the record.** Fork belongs with the clean-evidence group
  above: own self-play from a handcrafted bootstrap, Syzygy- and
  ChessTB-labelled endgame slices (exact retrograde results, no engine
  evaluation), Lichess CC0 openings. It has no Lc0, Stockfish or
  other-engine games anywhere in its lineage.
