# New evidence on known techniques

Part of the rare-ideas survey of 2026-11-15 (README.md). Techniques that already have a row in
docs/research/engine-survey-2026-11/comparison.md (A1-L4), docs/research/network-survey-2026-11/matrix.md or the
think tank's R1-R41, for which the groups found materially new evidence: LTC results, later SPRTs or
counter-evidence. Merged across the four groups; each row names the group(s) (G1-G4). Every figure is the engine's
own report; "a / b" is the engine's STC / LTC. Fork's status and results are from ideas.md, the Phase 4 SPRT log
and DESIGN.md at origin/phase-4. Every idea is **REFERENCE ONLY**.
